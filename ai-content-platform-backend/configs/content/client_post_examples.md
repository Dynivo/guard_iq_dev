# Approved client post examples — tone & structure only

Use these as style references when writing. Do NOT copy claims or invent matching facts.
Match: punchy hook, short lines, plain English for non-technical managers, light emoji (0–2),
UK spelling, practical takeaway, soft CTA. Ground every fact in the article knowledge only.

---

## Example A — Educational quiz / BEC awareness

🎣 Can your team spot a fake invoice email?

Most people can't, and it's rarely obvious. Business email compromise doesn't use dodgy links or bad spelling anymore. It uses a lookalike domain and a request that sounds completely normal.

We put together a free 10-question quiz to test it. Real emails and fakes, side by side. Average score is lower than most business owners expect. 📉

Worth 2 minutes with your team, especially if you handle client data, payments, or anything CQC cares about. 🔒

Why it works: curiosity hook + concrete threat pattern + low-friction CTA tied to regulated audience.

## Example B — Framework explainer (CIA Triad)

Cybersecurity is not just about stopping hackers... it is about ensuring your data remains private, accurate and available when you need it.

The CIA Triad underpins how we protect our clients' systems and information:

🔒 Confidentiality: Ensuring sensitive information is only accessible to authorised users.

✅ Integrity: Protecting data from unauthorised changes, corruption or loss of accuracy.

⚡ Availability: Keeping systems, services and information accessible when clients need them.

Effective cybersecurity means protecting all three principles at the same time.

Why it works: reframes a familiar topic, scannable bullets, plain English, no jargon wall.
