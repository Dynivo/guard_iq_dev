# Frontend

The frontend is the browser interface used to review news, create content, manage Guard IQ's brand settings, and monitor processing.

## News and ideas

- **News** pulls articles and shows their screening state and result.
- **Screen next 100** submits up to 100 unscored articles for screening. It must be selected again for the next batch.
- **Rescore relevant articles** rechecks existing relevant results without changing the normal unscored queue.
- Relevance controls let the user correct decisions and provide feedback for future screening.
- **Capture** saves a URL or a manual idea when no ingested article is required.

## Drafts and media

- Suitable articles and captured ideas can be sent to draft generation.
- **Drafts** supports editing, approval, copying, and exporting.
- **Images** and **Carousels** provide the visual assets associated with a draft.
- The Brand page controls whether images are generated automatically with a new draft.
- The blue and white image outputs remain separate styles and can be reviewed independently.

## Brand and configuration

- **Brand** contains the saved client profile, Guard IQ logo, colours, image defaults, and publishing targets.
- **Sources** controls which news sources are enabled and prioritised.
- **Providers** selects from the AI providers and models configured on the backend.
- **Prompts** exposes the organisation's supported prompt settings.
- **Learning** shows saved screening feedback and learned preferences.

## Planning and monitoring

- **Plan** shows approved content after it has been assigned a publication date.
- Publishing is manual; the tool does not post directly to LinkedIn.
- **Jobs** shows whether background work is queued, running, completed, or failed.
- **Analytics** summarises content and workflow activity.
- **Settings** provides organisation settings and the diagnostics export used for support.
